# Phase 7 — Why-Now Engine

The Why-Now engine produces an evidence-backed explanation of what changed, when it changed, why the change matters, why the timing is commercially relevant, the capability implicated, confidence and explicit unknowns.

It is deliberately deterministic at this layer. AI may later assist with wording or hypothesis generation, but the canonical assessment stores the underlying signal clusters, trajectory states and evidence references so every claim remains inspectable.
