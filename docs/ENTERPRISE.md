# Enterprise

Enterprise controls extend tenant administration, security, governance, availability and integrations without weakening tenant isolation.

## Capabilities
Potential enterprise capabilities include SSO/OIDC, stronger MFA, RBAC/custom roles, audit export, retention policies, IP/network restrictions, SCIM, enterprise policies, dedicated infrastructure and contractually supported service objectives.

Administrative visibility must be explicit and auditable. Enterprise availability objectives must not be advertised until monitoring, backup, recovery and operational response support them.
