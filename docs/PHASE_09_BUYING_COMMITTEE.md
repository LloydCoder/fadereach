# Phase 9 — Buying Committee Intelligence

Phase 9 identifies likely buying-committee roles from observable person attributes and evidence. Supported roles include economic buyer, champion, technical buyer, security, procurement, finance, influencer, blocker and unknown.

Role inference is probabilistic and evidence-linked. A title match alone is not treated as ground truth; confidence, role confidence, verification time and supporting/contradicting evidence are retained.

## Phase gate

Role inference, evidence linkage, confidence controls, contradiction support, tenant isolation, tests, documentation and all CI workflows must be green before Phase 10 starts.
