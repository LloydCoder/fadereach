# Legal Readiness

FadeReach is a technical product. Customer-facing legal documents require qualified legal review before publication.

Expected artifacts include Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Data Processing Agreement, Acceptable Use Policy, Subprocessor list and Cookie Policy where applicable.

Engineering must provide the controls those documents describe: identity, tenant isolation, audit, suppression, retention, deletion/export, security and incident handling.

Legal readiness is distinct from engineering readiness.
