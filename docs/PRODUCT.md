# FadeReach Product Definition

FadeReach is an AI outbound revenue OS that turns evidence-backed account intelligence into controlled, measurable conversations.

## Modes
- Tinlance internal engine
- SaaS workspaces
- Managed outbound service
- Agency/client workspaces

## Core loop
Research → Reason → Reach → Learn.

## Boundary
FadeReach is not a generic scraper, unrestricted email cannon, CRM replacement or autonomous-agent runtime. It is the intelligence-to-outbound execution layer with governance and measurement.

## Value
Find accounts that matter, understand why now, formulate a relevant angle, execute safely and learn from commercial outcomes.
