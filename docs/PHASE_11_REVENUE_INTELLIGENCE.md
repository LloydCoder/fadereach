# Phase 11 — Revenue Intelligence

Phase 11 closes the commercial lineage from signal and opportunity through campaign/person/message/reply/meeting/deal to recognized revenue. Revenue attribution is explicit, weighted and evidence-referenceable rather than an opaque last-touch number.

Daily metrics track opportunities, meetings, wins, revenue, sourced/influenced revenue and conversion ratios. Attribution is tenant-scoped and supports first-touch, last-touch, sourced, influenced and assisted semantics.

## Phase gate

Revenue lineage, weighted attribution, daily metrics, evidence references, tenant RLS, tests, documentation and all CI workflows must be green before Phase 12 starts.
