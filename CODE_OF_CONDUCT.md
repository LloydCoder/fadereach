# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, maintainers, and participants pledge to make participation in the FadeReach project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

We will act and communicate professionally, assume good faith while investigating disagreements, and focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and behavior rather than people.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and constructive;
- giving and receiving actionable feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting different technical approaches when evidence is incomplete;
- protecting private information and security-sensitive material.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, intimidation, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks;
- deliberate publication of private or sensitive information;
- sexualized conduct or unwanted sexual attention;
- trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith participation;
- attempting to bypass repository security, tenant isolation, or access controls;
- publishing vulnerability details or credentials in a public issue when a private reporting path is available.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Repository maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior and may take appropriate corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

The primary repository maintainer is **@LloydCoder**.

For conduct concerns that should not be public, contact the maintainer privately through the GitHub account associated with this repository and reference the relevant issue, pull request, or interaction. Do not disclose private personal information in a public issue.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will:

1. acknowledge a conduct report within 3 business days where a private contact channel is available;
2. review the report impartially and preserve relevant evidence;
3. avoid unnecessary disclosure of the reporter's identity;
4. communicate an outcome or status update when appropriate;
5. take proportionate action, which may include a warning, moderation, restriction, or removal from project participation.

Immediate safety or legal emergencies should be handled through the appropriate local authorities rather than this repository.
