# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not publish credentials, exploit details or sensitive proof-of-concept material in public issues.

Provide the affected component, impact, reproduction information that can be shared safely, and any mitigation already applied through the repository's private security-reporting mechanism when available.

## Response

Security reports are triaged by severity and potential tenant/data impact. Active incidents follow [Incident Response](docs/INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md).

## Scope

The application, deployment configuration, integrations and security-sensitive documentation are in scope. Third-party provider vulnerabilities should also be reported to the relevant provider when appropriate.

See [Security](docs/SECURITY.md) and [Threat Model](docs/THREAT_MODEL.md) for engineering controls.
